Ratings

Platinum
Everything we tried works as on a Mac, apart from what no app can do on Cutefish, such as iCloud or AppleScript.
Gold
Everything works, with one setting to change or small differences from the Mac.
Silver
Everyday use works, but a feature or two doesn't.
Bronze
The main task works, but important features are missing or rough.
Not usable yet
Starts and shows its work, but problems stop everyday use.
