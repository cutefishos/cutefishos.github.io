What comes with Cutefish, and what is up to you.

Cutefish is free, open-source software, with no accounts and nothing to sign. This page says in plain words what its licenses mean for you. The licenses themselves are what counts.

The software

Licenses
Cutefish's own code is open source, most of it under the BSD 3-Clause license. The About page lists the others, and each project's repository on GitHub states its license.
No warranty
Cutefish is provided as is, without warranty of any kind, as far as the law allows. We are not liable for loss or damage that comes from using it.
Software from others
Debian's packages, Chromium and the other software that comes with Cutefish keep their own licenses, and so do the apps you install.

Your use

Your responsibility
How you use Cutefish is up to you, and so is following the law where you are. Cutefish has no accounts and sends us nothing, so we don't know who uses it or how, and we can't control it.
Mac apps
Apps you run through Reef stay under their own licenses, and following them is up to you.

The Cutefish name

Anyone is free to fork Cutefish under its licenses. We ask that a fork or a modified build use a different name, and not present itself as the official Cutefish or CutefishOS. The official sources are cutefish.org, github.com/cutefishos and download.cutefish.org.

Legal questions? Write to hello@cutefish.org.
