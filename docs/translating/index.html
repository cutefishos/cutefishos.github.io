Adding a Language

Cutefish is written in US English. The project maintains sixteen catalogs itself: es, pt_BR, fr, de, ru, ja, ko, it, zh_CN, zh_TW, zh_HK, pl, cs, id, vi and ms. A wrong translation in one of them is corrected by a pull request against po/<code>.po; later updates keep the correction. Any other language comes in as a pull request, one per repository, and is welcome whether a person or an agent wrote it. The text lives in gettext catalogs:

Repository Template
cutefish-toolkit po/cutefish-toolkit.pot
cutefish-framework po/cutefish-framework.pot
cutefish-core po/cutefish-core.pot
cutefish-compositor po/cutefish-compositor.pot
cutefish-dm po/cutefish-dm.pot
filemanager po/cutefish-filemanager.pot
settings po/cutefish-settings.pot
terminal po/cutefish-terminal.pot
text-editor po/cutefish-text-editor.pot
video-player po/cutefish-video-player.pot
calendar po/cutefish-calendar.pot
system-monitor po/cutefish-system-monitor.pot
calculator po/cutefish-calculator.pot
preview po/cutefish-preview.pot
clock po/cutefish-clock.pot
disk-utility po/cutefish-disk-utility.pot
cutefish-installer po/cutefish-installer.pot

A language is po/<code>.po next to the template: ja.po, de.po, or with a territory where it matters, zh_CN.po, zh_TW.po, pt_BR.po. The catalogs already in po/ show the finished form.

  1. Make the catalog from the template, in each repository:

    msginit --no-translator --no-wrap -l ja_JP.UTF-8 \
    -i po/cutefish-system-monitor.pot -o po/ja.po

    Check its Plural-Forms header: msginit leaves a placeholder for some languages, Chinese among them; those without plural forms take nplurals=1; plural=0;.

  2. Translate every msgstr:

    • Keep each {name} placeholder exactly as it is written. Their order may change to suit the sentence.
    • Give every plural form (msgstr[0], msgstr[1], …) for languages that have them; {n} is the count.
    • msgctxt tells two uses of the same English apart, and #. TRANSLATORS: comments explain short or ambiguous text; neither is shown.
    • Desktop Keywords stay a list of words, each followed by ;.
    • Leave product names (Cutefish) and technical names (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, VPN, PID, MiB) as they are unless the language has its own form.
    • Leave Last-Translator and Language-Team empty; the commit names the translator.

  3. Check the catalogs and see them in use:

    cmake --build build/<repository> --target check-translations
sudo cmake --install build/<repository>

    check-translations runs msgfmt --check, which also rejects a missing or misspelt placeholder. Installed catalogs go to /usr/share/locale/<code>/LC_MESSAGES/. Pick the language in Settings > Language and drag it to the top: open windows switch to it at once.

When the English changes, maintainers update the templates with cmake --build build/<repository> --target update-translations, which also merges every catalog; entries whose English changed are marked fuzzy and show in English until they are translated again. Do not edit the .pot files by hand.
